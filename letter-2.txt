Dear Tee,

Hope this letter finds you well and you’re enjoying the beginning of a new season.

I am Gaston, and you don't know me, but our paths have crossed a few times over the past seven years. During COVID, we also had some email contact regarding books and subscriptions.

It started seven years ago, when the person I am dating were on a vacation in Edinburgh, we descended a few steps into your bookshop on a whim. The first thing you did was ask if we would like a cup of tea. (For context, this was at the end of November and beginning of December 2019.)

After this trip, that person and I understood we were entangled in something a bit more serious than several dates. Looking back, we realised that something had changed during our time in Edinburgh, and so we chose the 1st of December as our anniversary.

Two years ago, we travelled back to Edinburgh for our fifth anniversary, and there we had our first riso zine workshop with you, a practice I've kept up, though my partner hasn't.

The relationship we started seven years ago, and have been nurturing with care, has since its beginning orbited around books, that can be seen in the sheer number of books that have filled our little Amsterdam apartment since we moved in together, it has also been cultivated around nature, walks, hikes, and the discovery of cities through the people who live there, the communities they build, and the creative spaces they create.

Since then, Edinburgh has been a place we remember with joy. We return as often as we can, and have even thought about moving there. And we remember and talk about Typewronger with admiration and fondness. We love the place you have created, and how you curate its events.

It is for that reason, and a myriad of other ones, that this year during our anniversary I will propose to her.

You might be wondering what all this has to do with you, and don't worry, I'm getting there.

Somewhat unexpectedly, we'll be back in Edinburgh for our anniversary this year.

And my question to you, and the purpose of this not so short letter, is: could I ask her the question inside Typewronger?

There won't be a big spectacle, I probably won't even kneel (as I am overwhelmingly self-aware), but mostly a zine with a question and a "ring". I don't need photos, fanfare, a band or any spectators... besides whoever is at the store at the moment.

A zine I will design, riso print here in Amsterdam, and carry with me is all I'd need for the occasion.

I understand this might be a lot to ask, even if it seems small from my side.

And forgive my length and form, but this was the only way I thought of, as a total stranger, to put this question to you in the only way I know how: by overdoing it.

I look forward to hearing from you, in whatever way you think best.

With nothing more to add, and genuinely wishing you a good day,
