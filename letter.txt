Dear Tee,

Hope this letter finds you well and enjoying the beginning of this new season.

My name is Gaston, and you probably won't remember me, but our paths have crossed a few times over the past seven years, and I have a question for you, which has a bit of a backstory.

Seven years ago, when the person I was dating and I were on a vacation in Edinburgh, we descended the few steps into your bookshop. The first thing you did was ask us if we would like a cup of tea. For context, this was around the end of November and beginning of December 2019.

After this trip, this person and I realised we were entangled in something a bit more serious than several dates. Looking back, we realised that something had changed during our time in Edinburgh, and so we chose the 1st of December as our anniversary.

During COVID, I had some email contact with you regarding books and subscriptions, and two years ago, we travelled back to Edinburgh for our fifth anniversary. We had our first riso zine workshop with you then, and it inspired me to become active in a riso print club back home in Amsterdam.

Our relationship has orbited around books since its beginning. That can be seen in the sheer number of books that have filled our little Amsterdam apartment since we moved in together. But it has also been crafted around nature, walks, hikes, and the discovery of cities through the people who live there, the communities they build, and the creative spaces they create.

Since Edinburgh is a place we remember with joy, we return as much as we can, and we have even toyed with the idea of moving to your city. We remember and talk about Typewronger with admiration and fondness. We love the place you have created, and how you curate its events and agenda.

Somewhat unexpectedly, we'll be back in Edinburgh during our anniversary this year, and I have decided I will propose to her.

You might be wondering what all this has to do with you, and don't worry, I'm getting there: the purpose of this not so short letter is; could I ask her the question inside Typewronger?

There won't be a big spectacle. I probably won't even kneel (as I am overwhelmingly self-aware), but I plan to present her a zine with a question (which I will bring with me from Amsterdam) and a "ring". I don't need photos, fanfare, a band or any spectators... besides whoever is at the store at the moment.

The zine, which I will design and riso print here in Amsterdam, and carry with me, will be the only thing that is required for this occasion.

I understand this might be a lot to ask, even though it may seem small to me.

And forgive my length and form, but this was the only way I thought of, as a total stranger, to bring forth this question to you in the only way I know how: by overdoing it.

Looking forward to hear from you, In whatever way you think best.

Without more to add, and genuinely wishing you a good day,
Gaston Vazquez
