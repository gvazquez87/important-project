Dear Tee,

Hope this letter finds you well and enjoying the beginning of this new season.

My name is Gaston, and you don't know me, but we have had some contact in the past seven years.

Seven years ago, when the person I was dating and I were on a vacation in Edinburgh, we descended the few steps into your bookshop. The first thing you did was ask if we would like a cup of tea. For context, this was at the end of November and beginning of December 2019.

After this trip, that person and I understood we were entangled in something a bit more serious than several dates. Looking back, we realised that something had changed during our time in Edinburgh, and so we chose the 1st of December as our anniversary.

During COVID, we also had some email contact regarding books and subscriptions.

Two years ago, we travelled back to Edinburgh for our fifth anniversary, and there we had our first riso zine workshop with you. practice I have kept up, but my partner hasn't.

The relationship we started seven years ago, and have been nurturing with care, has since its beginning orbited around books. That can be seen in the sheer number of books our little Amsterdam apartment has since the moment we moved in together. But it has also been crafted around nature, walks, hikes, and the discovery of cities through the people who live there, the communities they build, and the creative spaces they create

Since then Edinburgh is a place we remember with joy, we return as much as we can, and we have even thought about moving to (but of course it won't happen). And we remember and talk about Typewronger with admiration and fondness. We love the place you have created, and how you guide its events and agenda.

It is for that reason, and a myriad of other ones, that this year during our anniversary I will propose to her.

You might be wondering what all this has to do with you, and don't worry, I'm getting there.

It was rather unplanned, but we will be again in Edinburgh during our anniversary this year.

And my question to you, and the purpose of this not so short letter is, could I ask her the question inside Typewronger?

There won't be a big spectacle, I probably won't even kneel (as I am overwhelmingly self-aware), but mostly a zine with a question and a "ring". I don't need photos, fanfare, a band or any spectators... besides whoever is at the store at the moment.

A zine I will design, riso print here in Amsterdam, and carry with me will be the only thing that is required for this occasion.

I understand this might be a lot to ask, even though it may seem small to me.

And forgive my length and form, but this was the only way I thought of, as a total stranger, to bring forth this question to you in the only way I know how, by overdoing it.

Looking forward to hear from you, in any way you think sensible.

Without more to add, and genuinely wishing you a good day,
Gaston Vazquez
